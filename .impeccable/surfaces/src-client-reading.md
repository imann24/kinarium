Mode: Read. Understand the community’s history and current conditions, or learn how the shared simulation works. Preserve the established content, disclosure behavior, and navigation.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Carry the festival identity through calm, legible reading surfaces within the existing information structure.

OWN-WORLD: Dela marks the page or dialog title. Manrope carries explanations, data, disclosure summaries, and inner headings. Semantic mint surfaces, ink text, and fine rules follow DESIGN.md in both themes.

STRUCTURE: Insights retain their existing summary and panel arrangement; the house narrative uses horizontal rules rather than an extra raised card. FINISH: Source styles and the corresponding light/dark desktop/phone review captures establish the current visual truth. No factual copy, data interpretation, or reading interactions are changed by the identity expansion.
