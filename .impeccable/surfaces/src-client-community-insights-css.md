# Community insights and field guide

Mode: Read. Understand current community conditions and recorded history, or learn how the shared simulation works. Preserve factual caveats, native disclosures and navigation.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Make the community legible through a calm reading hierarchy and open metric rows, with the house story as context.

OWN-WORLD: Manrope headings and explanations on semantic ivory or olive-charcoal surfaces. Sage groups supporting narrative; fine rules separate metrics and lists. DESIGN.md owns the shared palette and type roles.

STORY: Read the current summary, explore connections or residents, and inspect recorded activity. Optional explanations and relationship stories remain available without dominating the first view.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The title and scope selector lead into the house narrative and a ruled summary. Wide screens show four metrics; phones show full-width rows with label and value above their explanation. Connection groups and detailed lists follow. The field guide remains a dialog with bordered disclosure containers.

## Reading and responsive detail

The final interface layer makes summary and analysis panels flat and ruled. Four summary columns become two at (900px) and full-width rows at (540px); reading columns stack at (900px). The narrative uses a soft rounded surface, (15px)/(1.8) prose and a single column below (700px). Relationship prose has no decorative stripe. The connection-label explanation is a native disclosure; the underlying definitions remain unchanged.

The field guide retains bordered (12px) disclosure containers and a soft open heading, with (17px) Manrope summaries and (13.5px)/(1.75) paragraphs. Its dialog receives the common interface padding, elevation and backdrop treatment. Dela is absent from both operational reading surfaces. Legacy tiny metadata and eyebrow selectors are not reusable design rules.

No raster assets were introduced. The finish review covers the implemented light/dark and responsive surfaces; ongoing data and simulation behavior remain governed by their existing modules.
