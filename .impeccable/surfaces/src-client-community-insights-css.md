## Implemented community reading

The insights route caps its outer world page at 1480px and its reading body at 1280px, centered with 32px route gutters. The opening grid uses 1.4fr story and 1fr people columns with a 40px gap. The narrative sheet contains current atmosphere, individually dated retained moments, a visible coverage caveat and a native “About this reading” disclosure. A compact two-by-two ruled summary below it shows mood, belonging, pairs who have met and trust among met pairs. Statistics explain their denominator and empty states; scores describe current simulation state and are not goals.

People are sorted by lowest current belonging first. Each row uses the existing `Person` SVG renderer with that resident's actual appearance and color, plus their name, neighbors met, belonging and mood. Portraits are cropped views of existing artwork, not generated replacements; pair rows reuse the same renderer. Names wrap and away status remains explicit. Selecting a person opens their resident profile; selecting a connection opens the existing relationship dialog. The resident list is a labeled, keyboard-focusable scroll region (`role="region"`, `tabIndex={0}`), with a visible focus outline, 610px desktop height cap and contained overscroll. Scores and text remain readable independently of decorative meters and portraits.

Connection groups retain counts, text labels and `aria-pressed` selection beside the decorative distribution strip. Group buttons and the labeled native connection select control the same filter. Connections are sorted by encounter count, with a name tie-break, and use two columns above 850px. Initially only six matching connections are rendered. When more exist, “Show all N connections” expands the local list and “Show fewer connections” restores six; a visible count and `aria-expanded` / `aria-controls` describe the state. This is client-side progressive disclosure, not server pagination. Changing statistics scope or connection filter resets the expanded list. Scope and filter changes clear the selected pair. Scope, connection filter and selected pair are reflected in the insights URL; expansion is local state. The native connection-label explainer and relationship source disclosures remain reachable.

The statistics scope selects residents at home or everyone including away; it governs current statistics, resident rows and connection pairs. It does not change the whole-house narrative or whole-world retained activity. The narrative explicitly presents a selection: turning points from the last simulated day lead, followed by other selected retained moments from the last six simulation hours; atmosphere uses current togetherness and tension. Coverage can be incomplete and neither connections met nor the narrative explains a resident's feelings. Activity remains three two-hour simulation windows, based on at most 80 retained journal entries rather than the full archive. Missing records and skipped catch-up time can hide activity; zero means no retained records. Conversation and conflict counts are separate, friendship milestones are excluded, and historical score trends remain unavailable. Source modules own exact thresholds, selection logic and product facts.
