# Kinarium

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Product Purpose

A persistent multiplayer community playground. Residents move, converse, build relationships, and accumulate history. There are no objectives or win state.

## Users

Confirmed landing focus: curious visitors exploring a shared simulation, watching residents, and influencing a community together.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The landing route provides a searchable public directory, open-door and persistent-time filters, world creation, a field guide, and light/dark themes. Houses offer multiple templates defined in src/simulation/catalog.ts. Some require passwords; names and descriptions remain public. Everyone admitted has the same influence. There are no accounts or ownership privileges. Preserve these behaviors and the existing simulation renderer.

## Evidence on Hand

README.md, src/simulation/catalog.ts, the working world renderer, directory summaries, and template illustrations. No testimonials, user counts, or commercial claims are provided.

## Brand Commitments

Kinarium is the existing product name. The requested change is a less generic landing UI. No specific palette or typeface is pinned.

## Open Decisions

Discovering and entering houses is the primary landing action, confirmed by the user. Creation remains available.
